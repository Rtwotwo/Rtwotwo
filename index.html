Beyond a Single Human Demonstration: Learning from Scored Trajectory Alternatives for End-to-End Driving

Xinyao Rao, Zecheng Tang, Liuxuan Sun, Zheyuan Lai, Minjun Yu, Xun Cao, Wei Li

Scored alternatives turn one human demonstration into richer supervision for trajectory generation and refinement. Shared JEPA features provide temporal context for refinement and scoring without an online VLM.从单条人类示范拓展出带评分的替代轨迹，结合 JEPA 视频表征，学习轨迹生成、精炼与质量评估。

DriveTTO: Understanding Search and Selection in Test-Time Trajectory Optimization for End-to-End Driving

Xinyao Rao, Zecheng Tang, Zheyuan Lai, Liuxuan Sun, Minjun Yu, Xun Cao, Wei Li

Searching around a retained reference plan and comparing complete trajectories with frozen scorers. Both scorers check branch and fusion updates against the reference; the method recovers 51.35% of native zero-score cases on average.保留完整参考轨迹，在推理阶段进行端点搜索与局部精炼，再以冻结评分器比较完整轨迹。分支与融合更新须经双评分器检查；原生零分案例的平均恢复率达 51.35%。

Spark-H3: Adaptive Block Sparse Attention for Video Diffusion Models

Zecheng Tang, Jiaye Fu, Youtian Lin, Xinyao Rao, Liuxuan Sun, Jian Zhang, Yao Yao, Wei Li

Spark-Reblock groups tokens by attention preference; Spark-Reweight calibrates compressed attention. Together, they accelerate video diffusion without training, reaching 2.49× DiT speedup on MiniMax-H3 at 10% exact-block density.Spark-Reblock 按注意力偏好重组 token，Spark-Reweight 校准压缩块注意力；无需训练，在 MiniMax-H3、10% 精确块密度下实现 2.49× DiT 加速。
